# ModVala Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 May 2026  
**App:** ModVala - an automotive garage, trip, maintenance, budget, mods and community platform built for users in India.  
**Privacy contact and grievance channel:** pascoaldsouza28@gmail.com and valan.dj@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how ModVala collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you use the ModVala mobile application, website, community features, trip tracker, garage tools, account features and related services.

ModVala is operated from India. This policy is drafted for Indian users and is intended to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as applicable when in force for the relevant obligation, the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and Google Play user-data policy requirements. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Before public launch or investor diligence, ModVala should have this reviewed by a qualified lawyer in Maharashtra, India.

## 1. Who we are

ModVala is an automotive application for managing cars, bikes and auto-rickshaws. It helps users create vehicle profiles, record maintenance and expenses, upload garage media, track trips, discover mods, post to automotive communities, follow profiles, like and comment on posts, and manage premium features.

For privacy requests, ModVala acts as the entity deciding why and how personal data is processed for the app features described in this policy. In Indian privacy terminology, ModVala may be treated as a Data Fiduciary for personal data processed through the app, and its cloud vendors and service providers may act as Data Processors where they process data for ModVala.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect information only where it is needed for the app features you use, for safety, for legal compliance, or with your consent.

- **Account and sign-in data:** email address, username, authentication status, password hash handled through authentication services, OTP/reset flows and profile creation status.
- **Profile data:** name, profile photo, phone number if entered, address if entered, date of birth if entered, public/private profile choice, premium status and newsletter preference.
- **Identity and KYC-prep fields:** ID type such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence, and ID/RC fields if voluntarily entered.
- **Vehicle and garage data:** vehicle type, make, model, variant, nickname, fuel type, BHP, torque, weight, mileage, registration/number plate if entered, garage photos, mods, budget and maintenance logs.
- **RC/number-plate lookup data:** RC or vehicle number entered by you, and safe lookup results such as make, model, fuel or vehicle class if a lookup provider is available. ModVala does not need owner name, owner address, chassis number or other private owner data for this feature.
- **Photos, videos and media:** profile photo, vehicle photos, album photos/videos, community media, trip or captured-location photos/videos, thumbnails and related descriptions.
- **Community activity:** communities joined or created, posts submitted for approval, approved posts, likes, comments, follows, follower counts, reports, moderation actions and admin decisions.
- **Location and trip data:** current GPS location, trip start/stop points, route distance, elapsed time, labelled places such as Home, Work, restaurant, fuel pump, hotel or custom labels, destination searches and captured places.
- **Expense and maintenance data:** fuel, CNG, petrol, parking, washing, toll, fine, service, maintenance, rent/month for auto-rickshaw mode, trip fuel cost and service reminders.
- **Device, app and diagnostic data:** app version, device model, operating system, crash logs, performance logs, security events, deep-link payloads and network request diagnostics.
- **Payment and premium data:** subscription status, entitlement status, purchase identifiers from payment or subscription providers, renewal status and invoices/receipts where available.
- **Newsletter and marketing choices:** whether you opted in or out of ModVala promotional emails and product updates.

## 3. App permissions

ModVala asks for Android permissions only when needed for a feature you use.

- **Camera:** to take a profile photo, vehicle photo, album photo, trip photo or captured-location photo.
- **Photos, videos or media:** to let you select vehicle photos, profile photos, albums, community posts and trip media.
- **Location, including background location where enabled:** to track trips, calculate distance, auto-stop by destination, label places, calculate trip cost and attach optional location to posts.
- **Notifications:** to show trip tracking status, service reminders, community approvals, likes, comments, follows and important account alerts.
- **Microphone / voice assistant integration, where present:** to support voice-triggered expense or log entry flows through Android/assistant integrations.
- **Internet:** to sign in, sync profile data, upload community content, load images, use maps/search/fuel-rate providers and check premium status.

## 4. How we use information

ModVala uses information to create and secure accounts, keep each user's profile separate, save vehicle and garage data, upload and display media, moderate community content, send notifications, estimate fuel use and trip summaries, improve search results, provide premium features, detect abuse and comply with applicable law.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only where needed for app functionality, legal compliance, safety, or with your action/consent.

ModVala may use service providers for authentication, database, storage, hosting, notifications, payments, app analytics, crash reporting, maps/search, fuel-rate APIs and customer support. Community posts may be visible to other users depending on community and profile settings. ModVala admins and community admins may review, approve, reject, hide or delete content.

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, government request, cyber incident investigation, fraud prevention, user safety, or to protect ModVala's legal rights.

## 6. Community and public content

Your albums may be private to your account until you choose to post selected media or trips to a community. Posts may require admin approval before becoming visible.

If you make your social profile public, other users may see more of your profile and garage than they can see when it is private. A private profile limits what others can see, but content submitted to a community may still be visible once approved. Likes, comments and follows may be visible to other users.

## 7. Location and trip tracking

When you start a trip, ModVala may collect GPS coordinates, timestamps, distance, start/stop points, route gaps, labelled locations, destination data and captured-place notes. Android may show a system notification while location is being used.

Location data can be sensitive. ModVala should treat Home and Work labels carefully. If you post a trip or captured location to a community, the app should allow you to edit the public label and should avoid exposing exact Home or Work coordinates publicly.

Background tracking depends on Android permissions, device battery settings, app lifecycle and OEM restrictions. ModVala may store the last trip time and GPS location locally so an active trip can resume and calculate gaps when the app reopens.

## 8. Security

ModVala uses reasonable technical, operational and organisational measures to protect user data. These may include authenticated access, role-based/admin access controls, database row-level security, storage policies, HTTPS/TLS transport, permission controls, local-device storage protections and controlled access for admins.

No app, cloud system or network transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for using a strong password, keeping your email account secure, and avoiding posting private information publicly.

## 9. Retention and deletion

ModVala keeps personal data only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required for legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention, backup, dispute or compliance reasons.

You can request deletion of your ModVala account and associated profile data from inside the app where the delete-profile feature is available, or by emailing pascoaldsouza28@gmail.com with the subject "ModVala Account Deletion". Include the email/username connected to your account. For safety, we may ask you to verify ownership before deleting the account.

When your account is deleted, ModVala will delete or anonymise account data that is no longer needed. Some data may remain for limited periods in backups, moderation logs, payment records, legal records, fraud prevention systems or community integrity records.
