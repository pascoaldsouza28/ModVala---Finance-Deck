ModVala Community

Community Guidelines

Effective date: 11 May 2026

Core rule

ModVala is for respectful automotive enthusiasts, garage builders, riders, auto-rickshaw owners/operators and creators. Keep it useful, legal, safe and respectful.

Allowed content

  • Vehicle builds, garage photos, trip stories, maintenance logs and mod progress.
  • Auto-rickshaw business/running-cost posts inside eligible communities.
  • Helpful mod, maintenance, fuel, route, travel and cost discussions.
  • Honest reviews of captured places, restaurants, hotels, fuel pumps or scenic stops.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, hate, threats, bullying, abusive language or targeted attacks.
  • Illegal street racing, reckless driving promotion, evading police or unsafe stunts.
  • Sexual, explicit, violent, self-harm, exploitative or illegal content.
  • Posting someone else's private information, exact home/work location, IDs, phone numbers, RC details or owner data without permission.
  • Fake seller badges, counterfeit product promotion, scams or misleading pricing.
  • Spam, mass promotion, irrelevant links or automated engagement.
  • Copyright/trademark infringement or stolen photos/videos.

Blocking and reporting

ModVala may provide tools to block users, report user-generated content, and log moderation actions. Admins may use these records for Google Play review, safety investigations and legal compliance.

Moderation actions

ModVala admins and eligible community admins may approve, reject, hide, delete, edit labels, remove comments, remove members, ban users, restrict posting, verify sellers/mods and change community details.

Appeals and reports

To report content or appeal moderation, email pascoaldsouza28@gmail.com with the username, post/community name and reason.
